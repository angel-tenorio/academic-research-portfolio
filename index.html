PHYSICS, BIOPHYSICS & NEUROSCIENCE RESEARCH PORTFOLIO

Student Projects in Physics, Applied Science & Engineering

I am a recent high school graduate from Lima, Peru, with a strong interest in physics, mathematics, biophysics, and neuroscience. My advanced preparation in mathematics and physics encouraged me to ask how scientific principles can explain the behavior of materials and help solve practical problems.

In my projects, I have used wave propagation to study how ultrasound can locate features inside a material and applied mechanics to the design of a device for lifting boxes. These experiences have made me curious about how physics can help us understand both materials and living systems. I hope to explore those connections further as an undergraduate, especially through physics, biophysics, and neuroscience.

Selected Projects

Project 1 · Wave Physics & Materials Science

Study of the Propagation, Attenuation, and Diffraction of Ultrasonic Waves in Heat-Fused HDPE Pipe Joints Using the TOFD Ultrasonic Method for Preventive Failure Detection

An ultrasonic testing study that used time-of-flight diffraction to estimate the depths of four artificial reflectors in HDPE. It compares measured depths and signal arrival times with reference values and examines what the results imply for future inspection of heat-fused pipe joints.

Project 2 · Applied Mechanics & Ergonomics

Design and Sizing of a Pneumatic Vacuum Lifting and Handling Device with Integrated Weighing Sensors and a Path Recognition System for Boxes up to 10 kg.

A seminar design for handling boxes up to 10 kg with pneumatic vacuum assistance. It combines mechanical sizing with a proposed weighing interlock and path guidance system, and evaluates the design using calculations and projected costs.
